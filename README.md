# AI-Projects-OPIT

Seven projects from my Master's in Applied Data Science and AI at OPIT — NLP, computer vision, audio, and AI ethics — mostly graded top marks. Practical, varied, and deliberately spread across very different corners of applied AI rather than one narrow specialization.

## A thread through the projects

At the beginning of the Master's I read Jeannette Wing's *Computational Thinking* and that's stayed with me since. The idea: computational thinking — the method behind anything that gets built or developed — is itself a form of creativity. The same problem can be solved in genuinely different ways, and the way each person solves it says something about how they think. That's not a threat from automation. It's the opposite — the part of building things that AI racing to automate work doesn't actually touch.

Looking back at them, that's the thread. Different domains — surveillance policy, financial documents, smart-home commands, tweets, speech, language identification — but the same handful of instincts showing up every time: precision with language, attention to the human on the other end of the system, and an unwillingness to stop at "it technically works."

## 📂 Projects

**NLP & Transformer Fine-Tuning**
- [Fine-Tuning Transformer Models with Hugging Face Trainer](https://github.com/silviagara/AI-Projects-OPIT/blob/main/Sentiment-Analysis) — DistilBERT and TinyBERT on Yelp Reviews, with an honest account of where the smaller model fell short
- [QA Model Comparison on Financial Documents](https://github.com/silviagara/AI-Projects-OPIT/blob/main/QA-Model-Comparison) — tested whether a finance-specific model beats general-purpose ones on finance text (it didn't — general models won decisively)
- [Smart Home Assistant with Generative AI](https://github.com/silviagara/AI-Projects-OPIT/blob/main/Smart-Home-NLP-Assistant) — a GPT-2 command parser with a rule-based fallback underneath, so flexibility never comes at the cost of reliability

**Multimodal & Computer Vision**
- [Multimodal Sentiment Analysis on Twitter Posts](https://github.com/silviagara/AI-Projects-OPIT/blob/main/Multimodal-Sentiment-Analysis) — DistilBERT and ResNet18 fused into one model, because text and image alone each miss half the picture

**Audio & Speech**
- [Real-Time Speech Recognition App](https://github.com/silviagara/AI-Projects-OPIT/blob/main/Real-Time-Speech-Recognition-App) — Whisper wrapped in a Gradio interface, built to be used, not just run in a notebook
- [Multilingual Audio Classification](https://github.com/silviagara/AI-Projects-OPIT/blob/main/Multilingual-Audio-Classification) — language identification tested across five languages, using my own voice (Italian) as the native-speaker baseline

**Data Science & AI Ethics**
- [Global AI Surveillance Trends — Predictive Analysis](https://github.com/silviagara/AI-Projects-OPIT/blob/main/AI-Surveillance-EDA-and-Modeling) — built for the *Ethics and Regulation of Artificial Intelligence* course; where my background in international policy and applied AI actually meet
